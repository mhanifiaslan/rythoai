How it works

Three steps, all of them measured

Every link in the chain leaves a record on the server. There is no stage where someone decides "I think that one came from you".

01

Your link is created

We issue your code and your link. The shape is always the same:

https://rytho.app/r/YOURCODE
02

You share it, they install

The link sends them to Google Play and carries your code inside the store link. After the install the app reads that code itself — there is nothing for the user to type, and the app has no code field at all.

03

The first subscription opens a claim

When that user subscribes for the first time, your claim is recorded as pending. It settles 15 days after the purchase, if no refund arrives.

The reward

A fixed amount
per subscriber earned

No percentages, no tier table, no targets. One fixed amount for each subscriber you earn.

How is the amount set?

It is agreed with you and written onto your record. It can differ between partners, which is exactly why no figure is printed here — any single number would be wrong for someone.

If it changes later, does it apply backwards?

No. The amount is copied into the claim at the moment the claim opens. Changing it afterwards never re-prices your earlier claims.

Does the user get a discount or a bonus?

They don't, and we don't hide it: the programme does not change the price the user pays and gives them no credits or discount. Store pricing cannot be altered by a code. That is why you should not promise the user any advantage.

How many people can I bring?

Your code can carry a usage limit or an expiry date. Both are discussed in the agreement and stored on your record.

The life of a claim
  • Installed — the user arrived through your link and the attribution was written. Nothing is earned yet.
  • Pending — the user took their first subscription. The amount is frozen and the 15-day refund window starts.
  • Settled — 15 days passed with no refund. The amount joins what is payable to you.
  • Void — a refund landed inside the window, or the subscription ended early.

A user cancelling their subscription (turning off auto-renewal) does not void your claim — you did bring that subscriber.

The honest list

What earns you nothing

We write these down up front. A rule you only discover later makes the whole system look broken.

Anything that isn't a subscription

  • Credit packs. A one-off pack is not a subscription and pays nothing.
  • The trial itself. A trial period earns nothing. If the trial converts into a paid subscription, that moment counts.
  • Renewals. A subscriber's second or third month does not open a new reward.
  • The same user subscribing again. There is one claim per user — a structural rule, not a setting that can be loosened later.

Cases where no attribution exists

  • Your own account. A partner cannot use their own code on their own account; the endpoint refuses it.
  • Test purchases. Purchases made in the store's sandbox environment never count.
  • A code that arrived after the purchase. Attribution must exist before the purchase; past purchases are never attributed retroactively.
  • An inactive record. An inactive partner earns no new claims; settled history stays untouched.
A limit you should know

Attribution currently counts for Android installs only

Your code travels with Google Play's install referrer. There is no equivalent of that on iPhone or desktop, and since the app has no manual code field either, the code has nowhere to travel from there. So a link you send to an iPhone user may start an install, but it will not be attributed to you.

The link page tells the visitor the same thing: opened on a non-Android device it shows the store link and states plainly that the referral is not being counted. The iOS version is in preparation; this page will be updated when that path exists.

Payout

Manual, in Turkish lira,
on settled amounts

Settled claims are added to your balance; payouts are made manually in Turkish lira (₺) and written onto your record.

  • Your own numbers live in a partner panel behind a login: how many arrived, how many subscribed, and your pending and settled amounts. The panel is not public; access is bound to your partner record.
  • The payout schedule, any minimum and the payment channel are written into the agreement.
  • Every payout stays on your record as a line; the balance is settled minus paid.
  • What you earn depends on whether the people you bring subscribe; no amount is committed in advance.
When you talk about it

What we won't say
holds for you too

Rytho has one principle: nothing unmeasured is stated. It applies to our product copy and to your posts alike.

No health or treatment claims

Rytho is an entertainment and insight app. Words like "heals", "cures" or "treats" cannot be used; it gives no medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.

No certain-prophecy language

There is no "this will happen on that date" promise. The app interprets a computed sky; it claims no certain future.

No borrowed institutional authority

We name the source plainly: real astronomical ephemeris data (Swiss Ephemeris). No space agency or research institution may be implied to endorse Rytho.

Don't promise the user something that isn't there

Your link brings the user no discount, no bonus and no extra credits. Saying "use my link for a discount" is untrue and leaves the user disappointed.

Frequently asked

What partners ask

Does the user have to enter the code somewhere?

No. The code travels from the link to the store and from the store into the app on its own. The app has no code field, so you should never tell anyone to "enter the code after installing".

What if the app is already installed?

The link opens the app directly and the code is still recorded. It is never applied twice on the same device; attribution is written once per user.

What if one user opens two partners' links?

The first attribution stands. It is written once per user, and a later code does not replace it.

Can I see who the users are?

You cannot. Your panel shows numbers only: how many arrived, how many subscribed, and your pending and settled amounts. User identities and personal data are never shared with any partner.

Can I run paid ads?

Running paid ads in Rytho's name needs separate written approval — store and brand rules are our responsibility. Organic posts, content and one-to-one recommendations carry no such restriction.
