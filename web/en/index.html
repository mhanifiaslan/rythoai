The engine

We don't make it up.
We compute it.

Most astrology apps hand a few hundred canned paragraphs out to twelve signs. Rytho builds every reading from the real sky of the moment:

Swiss Ephemeris ephemeris data

Planetary positions come from ephemeris data at millisecond precision — not a lookup approximation.

True Solar Time

BaZi pillars are corrected for longitude and the equation of time. Month pillars are built from true Jie Qi solar terms, not from the calendar.

Honest randomness

The I Ching cast uses hardware entropy and reproduces the classical yarrow distribution — 1/16 · 5/16 · 7/16 · 3/16 — exactly. All 384 line texts are in place.

Readings grounded in sources

Interpretations draw on a knowledge base of public-domain classics and Rytho's own doctrine texts — not on whatever the model happens to remember.

Frequently asked

Good questions

I don't know my birth time — is it still useful?

Yes. The ascendant and houses need a time; Rytho states clearly that the time is unknown and makes no claims there. In BaZi the hour pillar is never built — it reads with three pillars.

What's in the free tier?

Your daily sign reading; the live sky screen; your birth chart wheel and placements; 5 chat messages a day; one person in your circle; the friends list and streak tracking. Forever, with no ads. New accounts also get their first 3 days on every Rytho+ surface without a card, plus a one-off 30 welcome tokens.

What happens to my data?

Your birth data is used only to produce your readings and is never shared for advertising. Face reading is measured on your device; the photo is never uploaded. You can delete your account instantly in the app. Details in the privacy policy.

Is this a fortune-telling app?

Rytho is an entertainment and insight app. It meets ancient traditions with real astronomical computation; it gives no medical, legal or financial advice and claims no certain futures.

When is it coming to iPhone?

The iOS version is in preparation. Android is on Google Play today; when you move to iOS with the same account, your chart and subscription come with you.
